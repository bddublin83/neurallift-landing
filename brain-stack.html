1
Ubiquinol (CoQ10)
Mitochondrial Energy Production & Antioxidant Protection

Powers the final step of cellular energy production in your mitochondria. After age 40 — or on a statin — your body loses the ability to convert standard CoQ10 into the active form. Ubiquinol skips that conversion entirely.

Dose
100–200mg
100mg if under 50 and not on statins; 200mg if over 50 or on a statin
Timing
With your largest meal
Morning or lunch preferred — fat-soluble, needs dietary fat
Food Pairing
Take with fat
Absorption drops 30–50% on empty stomach or with a low-fat meal
Pair With
PQQ
CoQ10 powers existing mitochondria; PQQ grows new ones
Why ubiquinol, not ubiquinone: Your body must convert standard CoQ10 (ubiquinone) to ubiquinol before it can be used. This conversion declines sharply with age and statin use. Virtually all commercial ubiquinol uses the same Kaneka ingredient — brand storytelling is irrelevant here. Dose and form are what matter.

2
PQQ
Mitochondrial Biogenesis — Growing New Mitochondria

The only supplement with strong evidence for stimulating the growth of entirely new mitochondria. CoQ10 optimizes the ones you have — PQQ actually makes more. Activates CREB and PGC-1α signaling involved in neuroprotection and long-term cognitive function.

Dose
10–20mg daily
10mg is the trial dose; 20mg for accelerated effect
Timing
Morning with food
Takes days to weeks to accumulate — consistency matters more than exact timing
Food Pairing
With or without food
No meaningful absorption difference — take with morning routine
Pair With
CoQ10
Complementary mechanisms — take together for full mitochondrial support
Why BioPQQ specifically: All published human trials used BioPQQ — produced by Mitsubishi Gas Chemical via bacterial fermentation. If a label does not specify BioPQQ, the ingredient is unverified. This is not interchangeable with generic PQQ.

3
ALCAR
Acetyl-L-Carnitine — Brain Fuel Transport + Acetylcholine Precursor

Two mechanisms in one molecule: shuttles fatty acids into mitochondria for energy, and donates directly to acetylcholine synthesis in the brain. Regular L-Carnitine cannot cross the blood-brain barrier. ALCAR can — and does both jobs.

Dose
500–1000mg daily
Start at 500mg; increase to 1000mg if well-tolerated after 2 weeks
Timing
Morning, before noon
Can be mildly stimulating — avoid taking after 2pm
Food Pairing
With or without food
Absorbed well either way; take with food if GI sensitivity
Pair With
B Complex
B vitamins support acetylcholine synthesis downstream
Why ALCAR, not L-Carnitine: L-Carnitine is primarily a fat-transport molecule for muscle and heart. It does not meaningfully cross the blood-brain barrier. ALCAR is the neurologically active form. The cognitive evidence base is built exclusively on ALCAR — they are not interchangeable.

Active B Complex
All 5 Vitamins in Bioavailable Form — Methylation & Neurotransmitter Synthesis

B vitamins run the methylation cycle — the engine behind neurotransmitter synthesis, DNA repair, and cellular energy. But inactive forms (the cheap ones in most supplements) require liver conversion that fails in ~40% of people due to the MTHFR genetic variant, or simply due to age and alcohol. Active forms bypass this entirely.

Dose
1 capsule daily
Follow label on chosen product
Timing
Morning with food
B vitamins can be mildly energizing — mornings work best
Food Pairing
Always with food
Reduces nausea from B6 and B12 at higher doses
Key Form Check
5 active forms required
Benfotiamine, R5P, P5P, 5-MTHF, Methylcobalamin
Why Life Extension wins here: Thorne Basic B uses folic acid (not methylfolate) and standard thiamine (not benfotiamine) — two meaningful gaps for brain optimization. Life Extension BioActive B-Complex uses all five active forms. This is the clearest brand differentiation in the entire stack — it is not close.

5
Fish Oil (EPA/DHA)
Neuroinflammation, Synaptic Function & Membrane Fluidity

EPA reduces neuroinflammation. DHA is a structural component of neuronal membranes — particularly at synapses. The biggest risk with fish oil is rancidity: rancid fish oil is pro-inflammatory, the exact opposite of the intended effect. Quality verification is non-negotiable.

Dose
1–2g EPA+DHA daily
Check label for actual EPA+DHA content, not total fish oil weight
Timing
With largest meal
Fat in the meal drives absorption; splits across meals reduces fishy aftertaste
Food Pairing
Always with dietary fat
Absorption up to 3x higher with a fat-containing meal vs. fasted
Avoid
Ethyl ester form
rTG form absorbs 70% better — check for "re-esterified triglyceride" on label
Why rTG form + IFOS certification: Most fish oils are ethyl ester (EE) — a less expensive form with significantly lower absorption. rTG form matches how omega-3s naturally occur in fish. IFOS (International Fish Oil Standards) is the only independent certification that specifically tests for oxidation. Nordic Naturals is widely marketed as premium but is not IFOS certified.

6
NAD+ Precursor (NR)
Nicotinamide Riboside — Sirtuin Activation & DNA Repair

NAD+ is the central electron carrier in metabolism and a required substrate for sirtuins — the longevity enzymes behind DNA repair, mitochondrial regulation, and stress resilience. NAD+ levels drop ~50% between age 40 and 60. NR is currently the better-supported precursor over NMN based on 2025-2026 trial data.

Dose
300mg NR daily
The dose used in published human trials showing 2x blood NAD+ increase
Timing
Morning
Aligns with circadian NAD+ rhythms — morning dosing is best studied
Food Pairing
With or without food
Absorption not meaningfully affected by food
NR vs NMN
NR currently preferred
2025 Nature Metabolism analysis: NR raised NAD+ 2x more than NMN in direct comparison
Why Tru Niagen (ChromaDex) sourcing matters: ChromaDex holds the primary NR patents and is the ingredient supplier for virtually all published human NR trials. Life Extension NAD+ Cell Regenerator uses Tru Niagen NR. This is a meaningful distinction — generic NR has not been independently validated in the same trials.

7
Magnesium L-Threonate
Magtein — Brain Magnesium, Synaptic Density & Executive Function

The only form of magnesium shown to raise magnesium levels specifically in the brain. Human RCTs show improvements in working memory and executive function in aging adults. Also supports sleep quality as a secondary benefit — making it the ideal evening supplement.

Dose
1500–2000mg MgT
Provides ~144mg elemental magnesium — different from the mg listed on other forms
Timing
Evening, before bed
The sleep-enhancing effect is a feature, not a side effect — take advantage of it
Food Pairing
With or without food
No meaningful absorption difference; avoid with high-calcium meals
Do Not Substitute
Glycinate is not equivalent
Glycinate is excellent for sleep but does NOT replicate the cognitive effects of MgT
Why Magtein specifically: The threonate ion acts as a transporter across the blood-brain barrier. This is the only magnesium form that raises brain (not just serum) magnesium in RCTs. The patented Magtein form was developed at MIT (Guosong Liu lab). Generic MgT products have not been independently validated — Magtein is not interchangeable with commodity magnesium threonate.

8
R-ALA
R-Alpha Lipoic Acid — Mitochondrial Antioxidant & Insulin Sensitivity

A mitochondrial antioxidant that neutralizes free radicals in both water-soluble and fat-soluble environments — uniquely versatile. Regenerates vitamins C, E, and glutathione. Crosses the blood-brain barrier. Also supports insulin sensitivity, which matters for brain glucose metabolism.

Dose
100–300mg daily
100mg sodium R-lipoate = roughly equivalent to 300mg racemic ALA in plasma levels
Timing
With a meal
Always take with food — can cause nausea or hypoglycemia-like symptoms fasted
Food Pairing
Always with food
This is the one supplement where taking fasted is genuinely not recommended
Do Not Substitute
Racemic ALA is not equivalent
The S-isomer in racemic ALA may compete with and reduce R-ALA uptake
Why Thorne wins here: Thorne uses sodium R-lipoate — the stabilized salt form of R-ALA that produces ~2x the plasma levels of racemic ALA at equivalent doses. Life Extension's default ALA products are often racemic (R+S). Check labels carefully if buying from other brands — most products do not specify, which means they are likely racemic.

9
Creatine Monohydrate
Creapure — ATP Replenishment & Cognitive Energy

Most known for athletic performance — but the evidence for cognitive benefit is strong and underappreciated. Creatine replenishes the fastest ATP regeneration pathway available. The brain is energetically expensive. Under sleep deprivation, high cognitive load, or mental fatigue, creatine supplementation measurably improves working memory and processing speed.

Dose
3–5g daily
No loading phase needed for cognitive use — consistent daily dosing is sufficient
Timing
Any time — be consistent
Creatine works by tissue saturation over time, not acute dosing — same time daily matters more than which time
Food Pairing
With carbohydrates
Insulin aids creatine uptake into muscle and brain tissue — take with a carb-containing meal or shake
Form
Monohydrate only
HCl, buffered, and ethyl ester forms are marketing — no RCT evidence of superiority
Why Creapure specifically: Creapure (AlzChem, Germany) is the quality standard — 99.99% pure creatine monohydrate, produced via a controlled synthesis process that eliminates impurities like dicyandiamide and dihydrotriazine. Many commodity creatine products from Asian manufacturers have failed independent purity testing. This is not brand storytelling — it is verified quality difference.
