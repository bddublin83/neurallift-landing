The SKLeTT Protocol

Specific Ketone Level Titration Therapy

What Is SKLeTT?

The SKLeTT Protocol is a physician-supervised program designed to achieve therapeutic ketosis—blood ketone levels ≥4.0 mmol/L—for brain energy optimization, mitochondrial biogenesis, and cognitive enhancement.

Unlike typical "keto diets" that aim for mild ketosis (1.5–2.5 mmol/L), SKLeTT targets the higher ketone range where significant neurological benefits occur, comparable to multi-day fasting but achieved through strategic nutrition and daily medical guidance.

Key Benefits at ≥4.0 mmol/L:
  • Mitochondrial biogenesis (new "cellular power plants")
  • 17–40% increase in BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor)
  • Reduced neuroinflammation via NLRP3 inhibition
  • Improved mental clarity, focus, and cognitive performance
  • Better sleep quality and REM restoration

Who Should Consider SKLeTT?

  • High-performers experiencing brain fog or mental fatigue
  • Individuals seeking cognitive optimization and peak mental performance
  • Patients with metabolic syndrome or insulin resistance
  • Those with treatment-resistant mood disorders (under psychiatric care)
  • People with family history of cognitive decline seeking prevention
Required Commitment:
  • 3 months of strict adherence to therapeutic protocol (typical duration)
  • Daily blood ketone monitoring and check-ins during induction phase
  • Daily reporting of ketone and glucose levels via text
  • Honesty about the timeline: Days 3–5 are the HARDEST; Week 4 you feel great

Key Protocol Elements

Nutrition Approach

SKLeTT nutrition is highly individualized based on your body composition, BMI, metabolic response, and ketone levels. There is no one-size-fits-all macro formula.

General principles:

  • Carbohydrates: As close to zero as physiologically possible (some constraints apply)
  • Protein: Titrated to your ketone response—varies by individual and adjusted throughout the protocol
  • Fat: Determined by your body composition and weight goals (higher body fat % = less dietary fat needed)

Your specific macros and meal guidelines are determined during consultation and adjusted daily based on your ketone readings.

Daily Accountability & Monitoring

This is what makes SKLeTT different: You'll text your ketone and glucose readings every morning along with your protocol day number. Dr. B.D. reviews these daily and adjusts your plan in real-time—ensuring you stay on track and troubleshooting issues immediately.

Required equipment:

  • Blood ketone meter and test strips (brand recommendations and complete shopping list provided after consultation)
  • Testing once daily (morning fasted readings)
  • Body composition scale for monitoring (recommended)
Important: Urine ketone strips (Ketostix) are NOT accurate for therapeutic ketosis. Blood testing is required.

Electrolyte Management

Electrolyte deficiency is the #1 cause of "keto flu" and early dropout. SKLeTT includes a comprehensive electrolyte protocol (details provided in your consultation and protocol documents).

Safety & Medical Supervision

⚠️ Physician Oversight Required If You Take:
  • Blood pressure medications: BP drops 15–25 mmHg within days; meds often reduced 50%+
  • Diabetes medications: Must be stopped Day 1 with physician approval to avoid dangerous hypoglycemia
  • Thyroid medications: Ketosis affects T3/T4 conversion; dose adjustments may be needed

Absolute Contraindications: Type 1 diabetes, pancreatitis, severe liver/kidney disease, pregnancy, active eating disorders.

Special Considerations: If you have a history of kidney stones or gallstones, these should be documented by ultrasound and discussed with your physician before starting. With proper management (adequate hydration for kidney stones; fat intake from animal protein for gallstones), Dr. B.D. has not observed complications in patients following the protocol.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is therapeutic ketosis safe? Won't ketones harm me?

Therapeutic ketosis (3–6 mmol/L) is safe under medical supervision. It is NOT diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA):

  • Therapeutic ketosis: 3–6 mmol/L, normal glucose (70–100 mg/dL), normal pH
  • DKA: >10 mmol/L, HIGH glucose (>250 mg/dL), acidic pH—medical emergency

Your body regulates ketones tightly when insulin is present. SKLeTT monitoring ensures you stay in the beneficial range.

2. Do I need to fast completely to reach 4.0+ mmol/L?

No extended fasting required. SKLeTT uses a specific nutrition strategy that includes some level of intermittent fasting (eating within a 4–8 hour window), but the exact approach is decided together based on your schedule, preferences, and response.

The protocol is individualized based on your BMI, body composition, and how your body responds. Most patients reach 4.0+ mmol/L by Week 3–4.

3. How long until I see results?

Days 3–5: Worst phase (glycogen depleted, ketones still low)

Day 7: Starting to improve

Day 14: Back to baseline energy; mental clarity improving

Week 3: Feeling noticeably better; ketones 3–4 mmol/L

Week 4+: Feel great—better focus, mood, sustained energy

Long-term: Mitochondrial benefits likely persist indefinitely unless you revert to old habits (sedentary lifestyle, high sugar intake).

4. Can I do this on my own without a doctor?

In 30 years of practice, Dr. B.D. has found that reaching and maintaining 4.0+ mmol/L is nearly impossible without daily physician guidance.

Two reasons supervision is essential:

  • Safety: Medication adjustments (especially diabetes and BP meds), electrolyte monitoring, and management of underlying conditions require physician oversight.
  • Feasibility: The rules are too strict and individualized. Even with written instructions, most people plateau at 2–3 mmol/L or give up. Daily check-ins and real-time adjustments are essential for success.

The only way to reliably reach 4.0+ without guidance is extended water fasting—which isn't sustainable for 3 months and carries its own risks.

5. Will I lose muscle on SKLeTT?

No—ketones actually protect muscle from breakdown (unlike medications such as Ozempic/Mounjaro which inhibit ketosis and allow muscle loss).

How SKLeTT preserves muscle:

  • Ketones have a muscle-sparing effect
  • We optimize and maximize protein intake for your individual needs
  • Resistance training is encouraged and gradually introduced
  • We monitor with body composition scales and labs

When done correctly under supervision, patients maintain or even gain lean mass while losing fat.

6. What if I have diabetes? Can I do SKLeTT?

Type 1: Absolute contraindication. High ketoacidosis risk.

Type 2: Often excellent candidates—BUT require very close supervision.

Critical requirement: If you take diabetes medications (especially insulin or sulfonylureas), these must be stopped on Day 1 of the protocol with your physician's approval to avoid dangerous hypoglycemia. Blood glucose drops rapidly—often within 48–72 hours.

Many Type 2 diabetics see dramatic improvements: HbA1c drops of 1–2 points over 3 months, reduced or eliminated medications, improved insulin sensitivity.

7. How do I measure ketone levels?

Blood ketone meter = REQUIRED for SKLeTT.

Blood testing measures β-hydroxybutyrate (BHB)—the primary therapeutic ketone—and is accurate at all stages of ketosis. This is essential for monitoring the 4.0+ mmol/L therapeutic range.

Urine strips (Ketostix): NOT suitable—they measure a different ketone (acetoacetate), become unreliable after Week 1, and can't accurately measure therapeutic levels.

Equipment details: Brand recommendations and complete shopping list (meter, strips, supplements) provided after consultation.
